Rate controls & regularity

σ measured over whole diastoles is biased by firing rate. These settings define a fixed-length window at a fixed phase of diastole (linear ramp removed) so σ is comparable across rates, and anchor the 1/fβ fit band to fbeat instead of a fixed Hz range.

The CV-IEI cutoff defines the periodic / irregular classification and must be reported in the manuscript methods along with the minimum-AP criterion for calling a column silent.
